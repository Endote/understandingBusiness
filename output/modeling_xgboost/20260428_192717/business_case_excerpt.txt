Forecasting

Source: Kearney
You were staffed on a project for a client who commissioned due diligence 
on a newspaper publisher - PaperRush
Market 
situation
– For several years, the market has been experiencing a declining trend of -10% year over year.
– Retailers' margins are about 15% of the newspaper's selling price.
– There is a significant difference between production costs and revenue from sales, which 
currently amounts to approximately five times the production cost.
– It is common practice to supply stores with significantly more copies of newspapers than actual 
demand requires to avoid stockouts.
Task and 
deliverables
– Your task is to verify whether it is possible to develop a model that can accurately predict the sales of 
each title in every store and whether implementing this model will improve the company's profitability.
– You need to prepare a report outlining the current state of the company, including newspaper sales 
and deliveries in previous years, the current financial situation, the sales distribution of individual titles, 
and the performance of different retail chains and store types.Additionally, the report should include an 
analysis of how implementing your model will impact the company's outlook.
Action
– To accurately model sales, you need to understand the client's product portfolio and identify which 
product and store characteristics influence sales levels.
– You will need to integrate data from various sources, conduct a feature importance analysis, apply 
appropriate models for different product groups, evaluate prediction accuracy, and estimate the financial 
impact.
